Graphs: Structure, Representation, and Traversal

This lesson introduces the graph data structure, covering directed and undirected graphs, adjacency matrix and adjacency list representations, and traversal algorithms including breadth-first search and depth-first search. Students will implement graph traversal in JavaScript.
