Applied Data Structures: Blockchain, Digital Currency, and Beyond

This lesson synthesizes the course by examining how data structures are combined in real-world systems such as blockchain, digital currency, and social networks. Students will trace how structures like hash tables, trees, and graphs underpin modern computing applications.
